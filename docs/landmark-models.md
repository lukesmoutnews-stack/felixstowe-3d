The pier deck is now level with the building's terrace (5.0 m ODN). Before, it started on the beach and ramped up. The deck is still walkable in the game, but in reality it has been closed since about 1999.

**Going inside:** the Spa Pavilion has real door openings, floors and walls. In third person the camera stays inside the room and under the ceiling. Seat rows block walking except along the rows and aisles, and the stage is reached by its side steps.

**Street View use:** three of the reference photos are Google Street View captures. They were used only by eye, to judge layout, colours and lettering. Nothing from them is copied into the game. Openly licensed photos (e.g. Geograph, CC BY-SA) are preferred for future models.

## Undercliff Road West shopfronts, 49 to 77 (Sept 2026 Street View)

`src/world/features/a22-undercliff-shops.js` (`shopFront` detail, set per building in `src/content/frontages.js`) draws the street face of Four Three Pizza (49), Nithin Convenience Store / Premier (51-53), The Grill (55-57), The Little Ice Cream Co (59-61), Life's a Beach (63), Rock Around The Shop (65), B.B.Q. Fish & Chips (67), Domino's (73) and GMG Collectables (77) from the photos in `docs/research/undercliff-road-west-2026`: window and door openings, mullions, stall risers, awnings, painted interiors behind the glass, printed food panels, the Premier and Rock Around fascia lettering, the two-tier B.B.Q. board, the cow-print panel, the banner on stanchions, the gabion baskets at 49. Positions are fractions of each unit's width (so they follow OSM's widths); no brand logos are drawn and the pictures are generic painted illustrations. The shop-name boards still come from `world/shopfronts.js`. The M.O.T. Centre under the flats is part of `flats-83.js`.

## Pelican crossing and the 79-83 workshop (Sept 2026 Street View)

- `src/world/pelican.js` draws every OSM `highway=crossing` + `crossing=traffic_signals` node: two rows of studs, a stop line on each approach, white zig-zags along both kerbs, red tactile paving, and navy signal poles on both pavements (traffic heads each way, a pedestrian head, a push-button box). The one on Undercliff Road West outside Rock Around The Shop (node 25685835) is the photographed one. The pier forecourt's guard railings (`forecourt.js`, `PELICAN_GAP`) are now open exactly at this crossing, as in the photos.
- `src/world/flats-83.js`: the wing is two storeys of white-clad flats (not three) over the ground-floor garage, with seven uneven bays, fins, balconies, a roof rail and only two end columns reaching the ground at the front (Sept 2026 wide photo). The undercroft shows a dark grille, the white "MOTs" panel, two green roller shutters, an open workshop bay with a lift, and the glazed RECEPTION office under its own roof, under a green fascia lettered Tyres & Alignment Centre / M.O.T. Centre / Clutch Centre / Brakes, Batteries, Exhausts (All Servicing & Repairs) / Reception. `wingLevelPlan` (added in `world.js` as the `flats-wing` terrain modifier) cuts and levels the ground under the garage so the floor, shutters and fascia stand on the ground instead of in the slope behind. The cars outside (a white van, red coupé, silver estates and hatchback, a green van, a blue hatchback) are real drivable fleet vehicles: the build returns `parked`, `world.fixedParked` holds them and `main.js` (`_fixedParked`) spawns them as `owner:'parked'` when you are within 110 m, so you can press E and take one (generic vehicles, no brand names).
