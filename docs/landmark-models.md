# Hand-modelled buildings

A few buildings are modelled individually instead of using the generic building generator. Each one keeps its OpenStreetMap footprint and position. The shape of each front comes from a photo the user supplied (October 2026). The models are built in code; no photo pixels or textures are copied.

| Building | Code | Footprint / height | Reference | What is modelled | Not modelled / approximate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Felixstowe Pier building (opened 2017) | `src/world/pier-building.js` | OSM way 91786049; EA LiDAR roof ≈ 10.7 m, top ≈ 11.9 m above ground | User photo of the front | Two white towers with dark louvred caps, flat lids and small domes; bowed roof fascia lettered FELIXSTOWE PIER; wide blue glazed panel; wavy rail; full-width canopy lettered FISH & CHIPS / ENTRANCE / ICE CREAM; shutter, open arcade and kiosk; terrace and 8 steps with navy railings; boardwalk around the building, level with the pier deck | The glazed panel is a generic blue mosaic, not the real artwork; the towers' monogram is left off; the wings are straight along the OSM footprint (the real ones appear to curve back); interior not modelled; tower spacing is taken from the photo (14 m) |

The pier deck is now level with the building's terrace (5.0 m ODN). Before, it started on the beach and ramped up. The deck is still walkable in the game, but in reality it has been closed since about 1999.

**Street View use:** three of the reference photos are Google Street View captures. They were used only by eye, to judge layout, colours and lettering. Nothing from them is copied into the game. Openly licensed photos (e.g. Geograph, CC BY-SA) are preferred for future models.
