# Playable zone and road closures

Felixstowe stays loaded as one town. Play is concentrated in the **town centre and seafront**, joined into one zone.
Every road leading out of that zone is physically closed with British temporary-roadworks kit, built from the
user's reference photos (ROAD CLOSED and ROAD CLOSED AHEAD signs, 750 mm traffic cones).

## How it works

- **Zone** (`src/world/zone.js`, data in `src/content/playable-zone.js`, generated by `tools/build-zone.mjs`).
  The zone is a band around real streets plus the shore. The band is built from street centrelines taken from the
  OSM road data, buffered 30–45 m to take in the buildings either side. The shore covers the beach, promenade and
  sea in front of Undercliff Road West, from the Leisure Centre.
  - Nothing in the map was moved or deleted. The closures sit on the existing road data.
  - Streets in the zone:
    - Hamilton Road, from Bent Hill to the High Road roundabout
    - Bent Hill
    - Wolsey Gardens, the one-way-legal route back down to the Town Hall
    - Convalescent Hill
    - Undercliff Road West, from the Leisure Centre junction
    - Hamilton Gardens, to past the Spa Pavilion
    - Orwell Road, east of Princes Road
    - Victoria Street
    - Cobbold Road, to Felix Road
    - the station car park, Railway Approach, and High Road West between Railway Approach and the roundabout
    - the promenade, from the Leisure Centre to about 230 m past the Spa Pavilion
- **Closures** (`src/world/roadworks.js`). Each road, path and service road that crosses the zone edge gets a
  closure a few metres inside the edge. Closures are kept at least ~10 m up the side road from the junction, so they
  never block the street that stays open.
  - **Roads:**
    - red-and-white barriers on black feet across the carriageway *and both pavements*;
    - a ROAD CLOSED A-frame sign facing approaching traffic;
    - a line of cones across the carriageway;
    - cones along both kerbs leading in;
    - a ROAD CLOSED AHEAD sign on the nearside verge about 28 m before the closure, where the road is long enough.
  - **Service roads:** barrier, sign and a cone line.
  - **Footpaths and the promenade:** barrier and a FOOTPATH CLOSED sign.
  - Barriers and signs have wall colliders. Cones have post colliders.
- **Soft boundary** (`main.js _keepInZone`). Between closures there is no road to close: back gardens, fields and
  the beach. There, a step from inside the zone to outside is undone and the message "This part of Felixstowe is
  closed for now…" is shown. It applies when walking and driving. Teleports and test placements outside the zone are
  not trapped. It only applies to the Felixstowe data (`data/` or `fx/`), not test regions.
- **Traffic and pedestrians.** The traffic graph only keeps road sections that lie inside the zone, checked every
  3 m, so AI cars never drive into a closure or out of the zone. Ambient passers-by stay in the zone.
- **Race to Be Mayor app** (`src/mayor/closed-areas.js`). Places outside the zone include Landguard Fort, the
  museum and the nature reserve, Martello Park, Felixstowe Ferry, Tidewrack Coffee at the Ferry and (since the
  launch closure) Breakwater Bikes on Sea Road.
  - They show as "Closed in the 3D town for now". Guide and travel explain the closure instead of moving the player.
  - Undiscovered ones can be revealed in the app, so the Landguard and Ferry trails stay playable.
  - Real-world check-ins are unchanged.

### Launch (9 Nov 2026): the town ends at the Leisure Centre

There are no finished buildings south-west or west of the Leisure Centre yet, so those roads are closed at the Leisure
Centre junction:

- **Sea Road** (and the Promenade, the beach and the sea beyond it, down to Manwick Road): ROAD CLOSED barrier across
  Sea Road about 12 m past the junction, a FOOTPATH CLOSED barrier across the Promenade, and barriers on the footpaths
  and the service road that run towards Sea Road.
- **Undercliff Road West** towards Garrison Lane: ROAD CLOSED barrier about 40 m west of the junction.
- **The lawn and beach between them** have no road to close, so a continuous line of the same red-and-white barriers
  (`fences` in `playable-zone.js`, built by `tools/build-zone.mjs` from the shore edge and by `roadworks.js`) runs from
  the Sea Road barrier across the lawn, over the Promenade and down the beach (134 m). Every 2 m section is a solid
  wall (segment collider).
- Breakwater Bikes (Sea Road, Race to Be Mayor) is now outside the zone, so the app shows it as "Closed in the 3D town
  for now". The Regal and the other buildings south-west of the Leisure Centre are in the closed part.
- To reopen a stretch later, extend its `box` in `tools/build-zone.mjs` (and the first point of `land`), rerun the
  tool and the tests.

## Routes that stay open

- **Seafront to town centre:** up Bent Hill (one-way uphill, as in OSM), and back down via Wolsey Gardens,
  Convalescent Hill and the Town Hall.
- **Along the seafront:** Undercliff Road West from the Leisure Centre to the Spa Pavilion and Hamilton Gardens.
- **Town-centre streets:** Hamilton Road to the Triangle, the station and the High Road roundabout, plus Orwell Road,
  Victoria Street and Cobbold Road.
- **On foot:** the promenade inside the zone, and all footpaths inside the zone.

## Verification (what was actually run)

- `node test/zone.test.mjs` (offline, OSM data): 292/292 checks passed.
  - Every one of the 88 zone-edge crossings has a closure whose barrier crosses the way.
  - No closure is in a junction.
  - The Triangle connects to the Town Hall, pier, Spa, Breakwater Bikes, station and every town-centre shop, by car
    (respecting one-way streets) and on foot.
  - Every town-centre and seafront location is inside the zone. The six others are outside.
- In the running game (headless Chromium):
  - Driving a car flat out at the York Road, Orwell Road and High Road West closures: stopped about 3 m short of the
    barrier each time.
  - Walking into the York Road closure, in the carriageway and on the pavement: stopped at the barrier.
  - Walking along the beach past the zone end: never left the zone.
  - Walking out from four spots just inside the edge: stopped at the edge, and the message showed.
  - 100 s of AI traffic: 575 samples from 17 vehicles, none outside the zone (after sampling edges every 3 m; an
    earlier version let 4 samples cut a corner on Ranelagh Road).
  - Mayor app at phone size: the closed note, Reveal, guide refusal and the pier's "Show in 3D" all work, with no
    page errors.
- The existing suites still pass: roads 8/8, bridges 4/4, mayor engine 28/28.

## Limitations

- The edge between closures is a soft boundary, which is an invisible stop with a message. Making every back garden
  physically closed would need fences along about 9 km of edge.
- Cars stop dead at the soft boundary rather than bouncing off it.
- AI cars can still clip a corner slightly when turning through a junction at the zone edge. No samples left the
  zone in the test.
- Closure positions come from OSM centrelines and widths. A barrier can stop short of a front-garden wall or overlap
  a driveway.
- Not every closure was looked at in the game. Five were screenshotted.
- This stage adds the closures and boundary. The extra detail pass for the two zones asked for in the brief (more
  shopfronts, street furniture, crossings, parking bays and visitors) is not done yet. What is there is the detail
  from earlier rounds.
